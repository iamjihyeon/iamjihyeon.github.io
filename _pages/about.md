I'm Jihyeon (Jennie) Kim, a platform engineer with a background in computer science and experience in large-scale data engineering.

My research interests lie in Human-Centered Computing, particularly in understanding human behavior and experiences through large-scale interaction data generated in digital environments. I am especially interested in applying computational approaches to questions related to health and well-being.

## Research Interests

My research interests focus on understanding human behavior and experiences through large-scale data generated by interactions in digital environments.

I am particularly interested in analyzing how people seek information, communicate, and engage with digital systems, including social media, online communities, and AI-based technologies. Through computational and statistical analysis of large-scale observational data, I aim to identify patterns in people's behaviors and better understand the intentions, needs, and experiences associated with them.

Health and well-being are application areas of particular interest, where understanding people's everyday interactions may help inform more personalized and human-centered support.
